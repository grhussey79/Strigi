Gaming Companion

An AI that remembers your play style, strategies, and the games that shaped you — and discusses them like a friend who was there.

The Problem

Gaming is cumulative. You build strategies over hundreds of hours. You develop preferences, discover play styles, form opinions about mechanics and stories. But AI gaming tools don't accumulate — they reset. You re-explain what games you like, re-establish your skill level, re-describe your play style every session. The companion that should be deepening your relationship with games is stuck at introduction level. It forgets the builds you perfected, the bosses that wrecked you, the ending that made you cry. You're not building a shared gaming history — you're having the same first conversation over and over.

What Your Palner Does Differently

  • Remembers your full gaming history across platforms and years
  • Knows your play style — stealth over combat, exploration over speedruns, story over mechanics
  • Connects games you've played — "this mechanic reminds me of what you loved in Outer Wilds"
  • Tracks your evolving taste — what you loved at 20 vs what you love now
  • Remembers the strategies that worked and the ones that spectacularly didn't
  • All your gaming preferences, histories, and discussions stay local

This is just one configuration.

One idea at heart: an AI that remembers you.
